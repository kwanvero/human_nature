🔢Questions to answer for effective cost analysis

  1. aHow much data. Rows times columns, for the chosen report only. Not the whole organisation.
  2. bHow often it is refreshed. Once a month is a very different bill from every hour, and almost always enough.

💵DRAFT Example cost calculation, based on assumptions

  • Data at rest: effectively free
    • The assumption. 10,000 young people, 187 columns each, 20 bytes a cell. That is far above anything stated anywhere in the pack.
    • The size that gives. About 37 MB.
    • What BigQuery gives away. “The first 10 GiB is free each month.” 10 GiB is about 10,700 MB.
    • So the BigQuery bill. $0. The dataset is using about a third of one percent of the free allowance.

  • Data in transit and in use: effectively free
    • What is free. “The first 1 TiB per month is free.”
    • What one monthly report costs against that. Scanning 37 MB once a month is about three ten-thousandths of one percent of the allowance.
    • What a busier version costs. A daily refresh read by twenty people a day still sits inside the free tier.
  • The dashboard: no licence
    “Data Studio is available at no charge for creators and report viewers.”

    • What it is. “The Free Trial gets you a $300 Welcome credit to spend over 90 days on various Google Cloud products and you won't be billed.”

    • What to do about it, in Google’s words. “You can help monitor and control costs by setting up budgets and alerts through the Google Cloud console.”
